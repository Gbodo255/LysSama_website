FAQ.

What's the difference between monthly and per project?

A project has an end date. We build the brand and the site, hand it over, and it's yours. Membership is for what comes after, or instead of it. $4,500 a month keeps one active request moving, and you pause any month you don't need us.

Is that the final price?

It's a real starting number, not a teaser. Scope is the only thing that moves it: an extra page, a second brand direction, a deadline that needs two of us. We lock the figure on the first call and it holds from there.

How long will it take?

Two to four weeks for a single piece of work, four to six for a full brand and site. We only take two clients a month, so your start date is real rather than a spot in a queue.

Who actually does the work?

The founder. You hire Mira, you get Mira. No juniors, no handoffs, no account manager relaying your feedback.

What do you need from me to start?

A short brief and someone who can make decisions. The clearer you are on the goal, the faster we move.

Do you offer support after launch?

Every build ships with 30 days of post-launch support. After that, membership keeps us on call at $4,500 a month. Cancel it the month you stop needing us.
