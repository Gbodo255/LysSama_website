Under Armour wanted its studio training line to feel like the practice, not the locker room.

timeline

5 weeks

scope

Brand Identity

Web Design

Framer Dev

Art Direction

The American performance brand. This engagement covered the launch of a women's studio training collection.

The Brief

One collection. One promise.

Training brands promise everything and deliver noise. This collection promises one thing: gear that disappears into the practice. The launch had to match that restraint.

View live

View live

View live

View live

The Approach

Show the practice, not the product.

The page leads with people mid-practice, not with feature bullets. Pilates on the reformer, breath work in the studio, the last rep of a hard set. The gear is the thread. The practice is the point.

Outcomes

Pre-orders sold out in five days.

First-run inventory cleared in five days. The waitlist for the second run grew by 4,000 in the first month. Three studio chains opened conversations about carrying the line.

The Word

Wren Beaumont

GM, Under Armour Studio

“ Mira understood that training has to feel like the practice, not the pitch. ”

Every pitch we heard leaned on optimization language. Jin was the only one who talked about calm. The page feels like a five-minute breath in the middle of a hard day.
