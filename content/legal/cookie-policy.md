## Types of Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies

These are required for the site to function properly:

- Session cookies to maintain your current visit
- Security cookies to protect against fraud
- Preference cookies to remember your cookie choices

### Analytics Cookies

With your consent, we use analytics cookies to understand how the site is used:

- Which pages are viewed most often
- How long visitors stay on each page
- Which links and CTAs get clicked
- Which projects and articles get the most attention

### Third-Party Cookies

A small number of trusted third-party services set cookies on our site:

- Framer for hosting and performance
- Google Analytics for anonymous usage analytics
- Vimeo or YouTube if a project page includes embedded video

### What We Do Not Use

We do not use:

- Advertising or retargeting cookies
- Cross-site tracking pixels
- Social media tracking cookies
- Third-party marketing cookies

## Managing Cookie Preferences

### Our Cookie Banner

The first time you visit the site, you will see a banner asking you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time via the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer.

### Browser Settings

You can also control cookies through your browser settings:

- Block all cookies (may affect site functionality)
- Block third-party cookies only
- Clear existing cookies from your device
- Set preferences for future cookie acceptance

### Cookie Retention

Different cookies are stored for different lengths of time:

- Session cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
- Preference cookies: Stored for up to 12 months
- Analytics cookies: Stored for up to 24 months

### Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in how we use cookies or new legal requirements. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of the page.
